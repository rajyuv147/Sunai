# Sunai privacy policy

**Short version: Sunai collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- Sunai uses the microphone only to play sound live into your earphones. Sound is processed
  in the phone's memory and is **never recorded or stored**.
- Sunai has **no internet permission**. It cannot send any data anywhere.
- Settings (volume, ear-test results, language) are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling
  the app deletes them.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.
- While listening, Android shows a notification so you always know the microphone is on.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page or contact the project maintainer.
